Calling notice

A VoRoute call has one job: ask if a cash-on-delivery order should ship.

Who is called

Only the phone number on that order. The number comes from the store’s checkout, not from a purchased list.

What is said

The call is in Hindi or Hinglish. It names the customer and the amount, then asks: “Kya aap yeh order confirm karna chahenge? Haan ya na.”

What it is not

It is not a promotional campaign or a survey. Attempts stop at three. The result is confirmed, declined, or unclear.
