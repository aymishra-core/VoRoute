Blueprint

The whole confirmation path, in order. Voline is the open slot. Nothing below it dials until that slot is filled.

  1. 01

    Order intake

    A store posts the COD order. The phone must be present and start with +.

    Ready
  2. 02 · voflow

    Confirmation job

    Builds the Hinglish script, queues the job, and allows up to three attempts.

    Ready
  3. 03 · voline

    Place the call

    Outbound call from the VoRoute number to the order’s phone. The spoken script is served back to the call.

    Waiting on Twilio
  4. 04 · vocore

    Voice

    Speech to text, intent, then speech. Hindi and Hinglish. Not ported yet.

    Later
  5. 05 · vowrap

    Result

    Confirmed, cancelled, address fix, or no answer. The store ships or holds from that.

    Defined

This container is the system. Dashboard and settings read it. They do not open the Twilio slot.
